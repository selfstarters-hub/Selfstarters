How joining
actually works

No mystery sign-up sheets taped to a door. Three steps, all online.

01 / BROWSE

Find a team that fits

Filter by category, check meeting times against your schedule, and see how many spots are actually left before you commit.

02 / APPLY

Tell them why you're in

A short form goes straight to the club's student lead — your name, grade, and a couple sentences on why you want in.

03 / SHOW UP

Get confirmed & go

The lead follows up with next steps — tryout, intro meeting, or just "see you Tuesday." Then you're on the roster.
