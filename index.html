How joining
actually works

Guests can explore everything. An account is only needed when you want to participate.

01 / BROWSE

Find something that fits

Filter clubs, check schedules, read details and see which opportunities still have room.

02 / SIGN IN

Create your student account

Use your email to create an account with your name and grade. Guests are never forced to log in just to browse.

03 / PARTICIPATE

Apply, RSVP & contribute

Apply to teams, RSVP to events, review clubs, pitch a new club or contact Selfstarters.
