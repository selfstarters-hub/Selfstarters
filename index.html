How Selfstarters
works.

A simple place for students to discover opportunities, participate, and eventually create communities of their own.

01 / DISCOVER

Find something worth joining

Browse clubs across technology, mathematics, chess, sports, arts, competitions, and more.

02 / JOIN

Apply and participate

Create an account, submit applications, RSVP to events, and become part of the communities you care about.

03 / LEAD

Build something yourself

Have an idea? Pitch your own club and eventually build a student community around it.
